Petros

Sales & stock control for container importers

Know what’s left. Know who owes you.

Petros is a sales and inventory system for traders who bring goods in by container and sell them on wholesale — for cash and on credit. It replaces the counting book.

Built for

Importers and wholesalers bringing in general goods — clothing, bedding, textiles, household — from suppliers abroad, and selling to market traders and shops.

What you get

01

Stock you can trust

Every item counted per supplier, across every container. Damage and shortages recorded as adjustments, so the figure stays honest.

02

Credit you can see

Deposits, balances and statements for every customer. They get an SMS the moment a payment is recorded.

03

Cash, MoMo and bank

Every sale recorded by how it was paid, so the day’s takings add up without adding them up.

How it works

1

Photograph the packing list

The items and quantities are read off the sheet and loaded for you. No typing in hundreds of lines.

2

Sell from the container or from stock

Cash, mobile money or bank. Or on credit, with a deposit taken at the counter and the balance tracked.

3

Check your position any time

Stock, debtors, daily cash and sales by supplier — on your phone, and exportable as PDF.

  • Stock adjustments for damage and loss
  • Bookkeeping built in — trial balance, profit & loss
  • Reports as PDF — sales, stock, customers, containers
  • Supplier and item price lists
  • One login per business — your data stays yours
